Research & projects

Diabetic retinopathy screening from retina photos

2026 · Medical imaging & deep learning

  • EfficientNet-B4 model that suggests whether a retina photo needs an eye doctor: AUC 0.983 on held-out APTOS photos and 0.949 at an outside hospital (IDRiD).
  • Designed a rim test that caught a later version reading camera clues instead of disease; built a demo app with Grad-CAM heatmaps.

VoiceRx: voice-based health screening

2026 · Machine learning · Track 3 poster, PRAVAH 2026, IIT Kharagpur

  • Three models screening short voice and cough recordings for respiratory, stress and Parkinson’s signals, with a referral-first design.
  • Found speaker-level data leakage in version 1 and am rebuilding version 2 with subject-level splits (GroupShuffleSplit, StratifiedGroupKFold).

Engineering immune checkpoint blockade nanobodies for cancer therapy

Narrative review · Submitted to Nanomedicine: NBM (Elsevier)

Review of nanobody engineering for checkpoint blockade: localised delivery, bispecific and multivalent constructs, nanoparticle conjugation, and gene- and RNA-based approaches.

VaakAI: AI speech coach
